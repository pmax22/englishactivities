Be, Have & Play

Présent simple · affirmatif, négatif, interrogatif

Rappel de la leçon

Au présent simple, trois verbes à connaître par cœur.

Be (être)

Ihe / she / ityou / we / they
+I am (I'm)he is (he's)they are (they're)
−I am nothe isn'tthey aren't
?Am I…?Is he…?Are they…?

Avec be, jamais de do / does : on ajoute not et on inverse sujet et verbe pour la question.

Have (avoir) et Play (jouer)

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
+I have · I playshe has · she plays
−I don't have · I don't playshe doesn't have · she doesn't play
?Do you have…? · Do you play…?Does she have…? · Does she play…?

Après don't / doesn't / do / does, le verbe reste en base verbale : He doesn't play football. He doesn't plays. · Does she have a dog? Does she has?
