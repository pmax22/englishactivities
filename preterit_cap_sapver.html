Le Prétérit Simple

Affirmatif · Négatif · Interrogatif — vocabulaire de la vente

Rappel de la leçon

Le prétérit sert à parler d'une action terminée dans le passé.

+ Affirmatif : sujet + verbe au prétérit.

Verbes réguliers : BV + -ED (help → helped, work → worked, prepare → prepared)
Verbes irréguliers : forme à apprendre (sell → sold, teach → taught)

I helped the customer. · She sold a dress.

− Négatif : sujet + didn't (did not) + BV.

I didn't help the customer. · She didn't sell a dress. She didn't sold.

? Interrogatif : Did + sujet + BV + ?

Did you help the customer? · Did she sell a dress?

DID porte déjà la marque du passé : après did / didn't, le verbe reste toujours en base verbale.

Attention au verbe be : I/he/she/it was · you/we/they were → négatif wasn't / weren't, question Was he…? / Were they…? (pas de did).
