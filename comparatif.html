Le Comparatif

(+) -er than / more … than · (=) as … as · (−) less … than

Rappel de la leçon

Le comparatif sert à comparer deux éléments. Than = que.

StructureExemple
+ plus … queadjectif court + -er than
adjectif long : more + adj + than		Hulk is stronger than Thor.
Gold is more expensive than silver.
= aussi … queas + adjectif + asBatman is as brave as Superman.
− moins … queless + adjectif + thanAn ant is less strong than an elephant.

Adjectifs courts (1 syllabe, ou 2 syllabes en -y) — attention à l'orthographe :

tall → taller · nice → nicer (-e : + r) · hot → hotter (consonne doublée) · heavy → heavier (-y → -ier)

Adjectifs longs (2 syllabes ou plus) : more interesting, more expensive, more popular…

Irréguliers : good → better · bad → worse
